# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to discuss prompt design, visual systems, and AI-assisted creative workflows. Participation must be respectful regardless of background, identity, experience level, geography, or preferred language.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and give actionable, specific feedback.
- Discuss ideas and artifacts, not personal worth.
- Respect rights, attribution, privacy, and project boundaries around supplied references.
- Welcome different design practices and explain disagreements plainly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Sharing another person's private information or unlicensed materials.
- Deliberately misrepresenting generated work, source rights, or security findings.
- Sustained disruption that prevents constructive collaboration.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel when available, or contact the maintainer through the repository's support channel. The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a participant according to severity and context.

This policy applies in Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces.
